Pull an image
Catalog is unavailable — you may not have permission to view it, or nothing is configured.
RHEL proper needs an active Red Hat subscription — Vigil cannot fetch it anonymously the way it fetches the catalog images above. Get the ISO yourself from a source you're entitled to use, then either upload it below or, if it's already hosted somewhere Vigil's server can reach, paste the URL and its SHA-256 instead. Legitimate source: Red Hat Customer Portal.
Windows images can't be imported yet — Vigil extracts a PXE kernel/initrd pair from the ISO, and a Windows installer needs a different pipeline (boot.wim/bootmgr) that doesn't exist here yet. Support for it is planned; until then, uploading or pulling a Windows ISO is refused rather than accepted and failed after the transfer.
Register an image
Streamed straight to the server and verified against the SHA-256 above — nothing else to trust or reach. Large ISOs can take a while; keep this tab open until it finishes. A reverse proxy in front of Vigil (nginx, Cloudflare Tunnel, ...) may cap upload size below what a full ISO needs — if the upload fails immediately, use the URL option below instead.
Before you paste a URL here: Vigil fetches this URL from inside your network, not from your browser — the request comes from the Vigil server, which can reach hosts, internal mirrors, and management networks your own workstation cannot. Point this only at a source you trust.
